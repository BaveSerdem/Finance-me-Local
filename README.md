# Finance-me-Local
Finance me Local is a simple, privacy-first app for tracking your spending and subscriptions. the app uses a zero-knowledge design—your financial info never leaves your device. No syncing, no hidden uploads. It doesn’t connect to the internet, doesn’t track you, and never sends data to any server. What happens on your phone stays on your phone.

 
# Finance me Local

A 100% offline, air-gapped personal finance and subscription manager with AES-256 encrypted local storage.

## Features

- Track income and expenses — fully offline, no accounts
- Manage recurring subscriptions with 8 billing cycles
- AES-256 encryption via PBKDF2-derived key from your password
- Biometric lock support
- 6 color palettes with colorblind-safe mode
- 6 language localizations (English, العربية, Русский, Deutsch, Kurdî, Türkçe)
- Encrypted backup export/import (`.vault` files)
